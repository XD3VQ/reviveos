Short, safe benchmarks

One CPU thread and small RAM copies for about 0.2 seconds each. Read up to 8 MiB of the Python executable, with no file writes. Worker timeout: six seconds. A brief load may be noticeable.

File-read speed includes cache effects; it is not raw drive speed. Results expire after ten minutes.
